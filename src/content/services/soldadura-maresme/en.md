## Welding for metal repairs in the Maresme

At Solbot Ferros, we carry out welding work in the Maresme to repair, reinforce or modify metal elements in homes, residential communities, businesses and outdoor spaces. We work with iron and steel to solve problems related to use, wear, oxidation or adaptation of existing pieces.

In this area, it is common to find grilles, railings, doors, enclosures, pergolas or structures exposed to humidity and sea salt. That is why we do not approach welding as an isolated intervention: we also assess the condition of the material, the function of the piece and the protection needed to extend its service life.

## Repairs due to rust, impacts or wear

When a metal element starts to rust, move or lose strength, it is best to act before the damage advances. We can clean and restore the affected area, reinforce the piece, replace a damaged section or fabricate a new custom solution.

Each case is studied according to the level of deterioration and the actual use of the element. Repairing an access door is not the same as reinforcing an outdoor railing or modifying a structure on a terrace.

## Welding for homes, gardens and communities

We work on common elements in homes and residential communities in the Maresme: grilles, fences, railings, enclosures, metal doors, supports, staircases and auxiliary structures. We also carry out modifications when a piece needs to be adapted to a new use or renovation.

When necessary, we take measurements on site, prepare the piece in the workshop and carry out the final installation to reduce intervention time and improve the finish.

## Direct service in the Maresme

We offer direct communication from the first enquiry. If you have a damaged metal piece, an outdoor structure with rust or an element you want to modify, we can help you assess whether it is better to repair, reinforce or fabricate a new piece.

Tell us about your case and we will advise you on the most suitable intervention.
