## Metalwork for homes and outdoor spaces in the Maresme

At Solbot Ferros, we carry out metalwork in the Maresme for homes, residential communities, gardens, terraces, estates and businesses. We fabricate custom iron and steel elements designed to be functional, strong and durable.

In this area, many pieces are installed outdoors and may be exposed to humidity, sea salt, rain or direct sunlight. That is why it is important to choose both the design and the finish carefully.

## Grilles, railings, doors and fences

We fabricate grilles, railings, metal doors, fences, enclosures, handrails and other elements for homes and outdoor spaces. Each piece is adapted to the real measurements, intended use and required level of safety or protection.

We can also modify or replace existing elements when they have become outdated, damaged or no longer meet new needs.

## Metal solutions for gardens and terraces

In homes across the Maresme, it is common to work on patios, gardens, terraces, access areas, swimming pools or outdoor spaces. We build functional metal pieces to organise, protect or improve these areas.

The goal is for each element to fit well into its surroundings, withstand daily use and have a suitable finish for outdoor conditions.

## Direct fabrication and suitable finishes

We work directly from measurement to installation. Depending on where the piece will be installed, we can advise on primers, paint, galvanising or other finishes to improve resistance to outdoor exposure.

If you need to fabricate, repair or install a metalwork element in the Maresme, we can help you define a custom solution.
