## Custom metalwork for homes, businesses and residential communities

At Solbot Ferros we fabricate and install iron and steel metalwork for private clients, residential communities, businesses, renovations and technical projects. Every element is custom-made to fit the space, intended use and desired finish.

We manufacture security grilles, metal railings, fences, gates, enclosures, indoor and outdoor staircases and other bespoke metal elements.

## Railings, fences, grilles and metal gates

Metalwork provides durable solutions for security, access control, space delimitation and architectural integration. We fabricate fixed and opening grilles, balcony railings, perimeter fencing, metal gates and custom enclosures.

Each project is adapted to the environment, required security level, anchoring system and suitable finish for indoor or outdoor use.

## Metal staircases and custom solutions

We also build indoor and outdoor metal staircases for homes, commercial spaces and technical environments. Structures can be combined with wood, sheet metal or other materials depending on the project requirements.

In addition, we carry out modifications, reinforcements and repairs of existing metal elements when functionality, safety or appearance needs to be improved.

## Service in Barcelona, Maresme and Vallès

We work across Barcelona, Maresme and Vallès, providing custom fabrication and professional installation. We can visit the site, take measurements and advise you on materials, finishes and construction solutions.
