## Custom metalwork in Barcelona

At Solbot Ferros, we carry out metalwork in Barcelona for homes, residential communities, commercial premises, renovation works and professional projects. We fabricate custom iron and steel elements adapted to each space and use.

We work on functional, strong and durable pieces: grilles, railings, doors, enclosures, staircases, handrails and personalised metal solutions.

## Grilles, railings, doors and enclosures

Metalwork helps solve needs related to safety, access, protection and design. We fabricate window grilles, balcony or staircase railings, metal doors, enclosures and auxiliary elements for homes or businesses.

Each job is adapted to the real measurements of the space, the intended use and the desired finish.

## Work for communities, premises and renovations

In Barcelona, it is common to work in existing buildings, residential communities, commercial premises or homes undergoing renovation. In these cases, planning is important to reduce disruption, coordinate access and carry out the installation in an organised way.

We can work directly with the client or coordinate with renovation companies, architects, interior designers or other professionals.

## Direct fabrication and installation

We manage the process from measurement to final installation. This allows us to control fabrication, adjust details and offer a solution that fits the space properly.

If you need a custom metal piece in Barcelona, we can help you define the most suitable option.
