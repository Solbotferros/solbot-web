## Custom metal structures for residential, commercial and industrial projects

At Solbot Ferros we design, fabricate and install metal structures for homes, businesses, hospitality projects, renovations and industrial environments. We work with iron and steel to create durable and functional solutions tailored to each project.

We build pergolas, canopies, carports, terrace enclosures, beams, supports and structural reinforcements, always adapting the structure to the available space, intended use and installation conditions.

## Pergolas, canopies and outdoor structures

Metal structures are a durable solution for covering, protecting or extending outdoor spaces. We manufacture pergolas, canopies and metal enclosures for terraces, gardens, entrances, patios and hospitality spaces.

Each structure is designed according to the actual dimensions, type of covering, wind exposure, architectural style and finish required to ensure both durability and visual integration.

## Structural reinforcements and technical work

We also carry out structural reinforcement projects using beams, pillars, supports and other metal elements. These interventions require precision in fabrication, technical judgment and safe installation.

When needed, we work following technical specifications or architectural direction, especially in renovations, extensions and industrial projects.

## Service in Barcelona, Maresme and Vallès

We work across Barcelona, Maresme and Vallès, adapting each project to the specific conditions of the location. We can carry out technical visits, take measurements and prepare a tailored proposal based on the intended use and required finish.
