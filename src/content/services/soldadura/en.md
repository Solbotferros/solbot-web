## Professional welding for structures, repairs and metal modifications

At Solbot Ferros we provide welding services for private clients, businesses, residential communities, renovations and industry. We work on both new fabrication projects and repairs of existing metal elements.

We work with iron and steel to solve needs such as structural reinforcements, railing repairs, replacement of rusted parts, modification of metal doors or welding of elements damaged by daily use.

## Common welding works

Our welding services include punctual repairs, workshop fabrication and on-site interventions when the project requires it. Before starting any work, we assess the condition of the piece, accessibility, material type and required finish to ensure a strong, functional and durable result.

## Repair of rusted or damaged elements

Oxidation is common in outdoor metal structures, especially in coastal areas such as Maresme. When a piece shows rust, looseness or structural weakness, we evaluate whether it should be repaired, reinforced or replaced with a new metal element.

## Service in Barcelona, Maresme and Vallès

We work across Barcelona, Maresme and Vallès with direct service and no intermediaries. We can carry out technical visits, take measurements and recommend the best solution depending on the type of work, location and urgency.
