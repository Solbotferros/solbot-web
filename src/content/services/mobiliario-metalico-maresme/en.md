## Custom metal furniture in the Maresme

At Solbot Ferros, we fabricate metal furniture in the Maresme for homes, gardens, terraces, restaurants, businesses and personalised projects. We create custom iron and steel pieces designed to withstand daily use and adapt to the style of each space.

Metal furniture is especially useful when looking for a strong, stable and durable piece: tables, benches, shelves, bars, supports, decorative structures or special furniture.

## Metal furniture for terraces, gardens and outdoor spaces

In the Maresme, many pieces are used in outdoor spaces, where they are exposed to humidity, sun, rain or coastal conditions. That is why it is important to choose the design, proportions and finish carefully.

We fabricate metal furniture for terraces, gardens, patios, porches, pool areas, restaurants and outdoor spaces in homes or businesses.

## Solutions for homes, businesses and hospitality

We also work on indoor furniture: tables, shelves, benches, supports, bars, auxiliary structures and personalised metal pieces. Each element is planned according to its use, the available measurements and the desired finish.

In restaurants, shops and businesses, furniture must be functional, strong and consistent with the image of the space.

## Direct fabrication and durable finishes

We manage the process from the idea and measurements to final fabrication. We can advise on paint, protective treatments and combinations with other materials.

If you need custom metal furniture in the Maresme, we can help you design a functional, durable and well-integrated piece.
