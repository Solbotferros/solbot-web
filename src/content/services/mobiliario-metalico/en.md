## Custom metal furniture for homes, businesses and hospitality spaces

At Solbot Ferros we fabricate custom metal furniture for interiors, outdoor spaces, hospitality venues, businesses and residential projects. We create durable and functional pieces adapted to the style and needs of each space.

We work with iron, steel and combinations with wood to manufacture tables, shelving, room dividers, counters, benches, barbecues, planters, mailboxes and custom metal signage.

## Bespoke metal furniture design

Metal furniture allows the creation of durable pieces with clean lines and a high level of customisation. We fabricate iron and wood furniture, shelving systems, benches, coat racks and other made-to-measure elements.

Each piece is designed according to the available space, intended use, structural requirements and desired finish.

## Furniture for hospitality and commercial spaces

We also work with bars, restaurants, terraces, retail spaces and professional environments. We fabricate counters, tables, stools, shelving, partitions and custom metal signage.

These elements must combine durability, ease of maintenance and visual consistency with the identity of the business.

## Service in Barcelona, Maresme and Vallès

We provide custom metal furniture services across Barcelona, Maresme and Vallès. We can advise you on dimensions, materials, finishes and construction solutions to achieve a durable and well-integrated final result.
