## Welding for repairs, reinforcements and metalwork in Barcelona

At Solbot Ferros, we provide welding services in Barcelona to solve real needs in homes, residential communities, commercial premises, renovation companies and small industrial projects. We work with iron and steel to repair, reinforce, modify or fabricate custom metal elements.

Our approach is direct: we assess the job, study the piece or structure, propose the most suitable solution and carry out the repair or fabrication without intermediaries. This allows us to better control the quality of the result, the timing and the final finish.

## Repair of oxidised or damaged elements

Oxidation is one of the most common problems in outdoor metal elements. When a grille, railing, door or structure starts to show rust, looseness or loss of rigidity, it is best to act before the deterioration advances.

Depending on the condition of the piece, we can clean and restore the affected area, reinforce the element, replace a damaged part or fabricate a new piece. The solution depends on the level of corrosion, the function of the element and whether it is decorative, safety-related or structural.

## Fabrication and modification of metal parts

In addition to repairs, we also fabricate custom metal parts. We can create supports, reinforcements, frames, auxiliary structures, doors, enclosures or specific elements to solve a particular need.

Each job is planned according to the actual use of the piece: a one-off repair on a metal door is not the same as a reinforcement for a load-bearing structure or a railing that must fulfil a safety function.

## Direct welding service in Barcelona

We work in Barcelona and the surrounding areas, offering direct communication from the first enquiry. If you have a damaged metal piece, a structure that needs reinforcement or an element you want to modify, we can help you assess the best solution.
