## Metal structures for homes and outdoor spaces in the Maresme

At Solbot Ferros, we fabricate metal structures in the Maresme for homes, residential communities, gardens, terraces, estates and outdoor projects. We work with custom solutions that combine strength, functionality and suitable finishes for each environment.

In this area, many structures are exposed to humidity, sea salt, rain, sun and temperature changes. That is why it is important to think not only about the shape of the structure, but also about its protection, drainage, maintenance and durability.

## Pergolas, supports and auxiliary structures

We build metal pergolas, supports, staircases, platforms, enclosures, auxiliary structures and reinforcements for outdoor spaces. Each piece is fabricated according to measurements, intended use and installation conditions.

When the project requires it, we study how the structure will integrate with existing elements: walls, paving, façades, access areas or garden spaces.

## Resistance and protection against coastal conditions

In the Maresme, the coastal environment can accelerate the wear of metal structures if they are not properly planned from the beginning. Good fabrication should be combined with a suitable finish and a design that avoids water accumulation or critical oxidation points.

That is why we recommend treatments and finishes according to the real exposure of each piece.

## Direct fabrication and installation

We work directly from measurement to installation. This allows us to better adjust the structure, control the finish and resolve technical details before final installation.

If you need a metal structure for a home, garden, terrace or residential community in the Maresme, we can help you define a strong solution adapted to the environment.
