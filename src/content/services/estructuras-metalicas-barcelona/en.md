## Fabrication and installation of metal structures in Barcelona

At Solbot Ferros, we build metal structures in Barcelona for homes, residential communities, commercial premises, renovations and professional projects. We fabricate custom solutions to reinforce, adapt or build metal elements according to the real needs of each space.

Each project starts with a technical assessment: measurements, intended use, access conditions, type of installation and final finish. This step is important to avoid improvisation and achieve a strong, functional and well-integrated structure.

## Reinforcements and structures for renovations

In renovations and existing buildings, metal structures are often used to reinforce elements, create new supports, solve layout changes or adapt a space to a new use. We work with iron and steel to fabricate robust pieces adjusted to the project.

When the intervention requires it, we coordinate workshop fabrication with on-site installation to reduce installation time and make coordination with other professionals easier.

## Metal solutions for communities and premises

We also build auxiliary structures, staircases, platforms, supports, frames, enclosures and metal elements for residential communities, commercial premises and technical spaces.

In Barcelona, where many interventions take place in occupied buildings or operating premises, we pay special attention to planning, clean installation and access coordination.

## Custom metal structures

We do not work with standard solutions when the project requires a specific piece. We adapt each structure to the measurements, use, required strength and intended finish.

If you need to fabricate, reinforce or modify a metal structure in Barcelona, we can help you study the most suitable solution.
