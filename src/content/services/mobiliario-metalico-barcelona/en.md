## Custom metal furniture in Barcelona

At Solbot Ferros, we fabricate metal furniture in Barcelona for homes, commercial premises, restaurants, offices and interior design projects. We work with iron and steel to create strong, functional pieces adapted to the style of each space.

Metal furniture makes it possible to solve practical needs without giving up design: tables, shelves, bars, benches, decorative structures, supports and special custom-made pieces.

## Metal furniture for premises, restaurants and offices

In commercial spaces, furniture must be strong, functional and consistent with the image of the business. We fabricate metal pieces for bars, service areas, shelves, dividers, tables, supports and auxiliary elements.

Each project is planned according to the real use of the piece, the flow of the space, the available measurements and the desired finish.

## Metal pieces for homes and interior design

We also fabricate metal furniture for homes and interior design projects: dining tables, side tables, bookcases, shelves, benches, coat racks, supports and decorative structures.

The goal is to create durable, well-proportioned pieces adapted to the space, avoiding standard solutions when the project requires something specific.

## Direct fabrication and custom finishes

We work directly from the initial idea to final fabrication. We can advise on proportions, strength, finishes, paint and possible combinations with other materials.

If you need custom metal furniture in Barcelona, we can help you define a functional solution with character.
