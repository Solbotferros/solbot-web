In this project, we carried out the fabrication and installation of a custom metal enclosure for an animal area in Granollers.

The aim was to create a safe, resistant and easy-to-maintain environment that would delimit the space without losing ventilation or visibility. For this reason, a metal structure combined with mesh panels was chosen, ensuring robustness and practical day-to-day use.

Access points were designed using metal doors integrated into the enclosure, making entry and maintenance easier. Special care was also taken with the stability of the structure and the correct fixing of all elements, ensuring reliable performance under continuous use.

The result is a functional and durable enclosure, perfectly adapted to its purpose, offering safety and convenience both for the animals and for maintenance work.
