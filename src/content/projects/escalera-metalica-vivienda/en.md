In this project, we carried out the design, fabrication and installation of a custom interior metal staircase for a home in Barcelona.

The aim was to connect two levels of the home with a functional solution that would also fit the style of the space without visually overloading it. For this reason, a staircase with clean lines and a contemporary aesthetic was proposed, prioritising visual lightness without compromising structural solidity.

The structure was made entirely of steel, adapting each element to the actual measurements of the home to ensure a precise on-site fit. Special attention was paid to aspects such as ease of use, stability and finishes, aiming for a durable result designed for everyday use.

Beyond its function, the staircase becomes part of the space as a key feature, adding character and coherence to the overall home design.
