In this project, we carried out the design, fabrication and installation of a custom metal railing for the balcony of a home in Cabrils.

The client was looking for a safe and durable solution for an elevated balcony, without giving up a clean aesthetic that would not interfere with the views of the surroundings. For this reason, a vertical-bar design was chosen, providing visual lightness, keeping the space open and meeting the required safety standards.

The structure was fabricated in steel, adapting each section to the exact measurements of the balcony to ensure a precise on-site fit. Special attention was paid to the rigidity of the whole structure, its fixing to the existing construction and the quality of the finishes, also taking into account its exposure to an environment close to the sea.

The result is a functional and discreet metal railing, perfectly integrated into the home, reinforcing the safety of the space without compromising its aesthetics or views.
