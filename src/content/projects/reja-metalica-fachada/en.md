In this project, we carried out the fabrication and installation of a custom metal security grille for a façade in Barcelona.

Unlike traditional grilles, the client was looking for a more contemporary design that would provide security without creating an overly closed or heavy appearance. For this reason, a pattern of vertical bars combined with horizontal reinforcements was developed, creating a more dynamic and visually light composition.

The piece was fabricated entirely in the workshop, with careful attention paid to the alignment of all elements and the quality of the welds, before being installed to fit the existing opening. This type of work requires precision both when taking measurements and during execution, as any deviation directly affects the final result on the façade.

The result is a robust and secure metal grille with a contemporary design that integrates perfectly with the building’s aesthetics and improves protection without compromising the overall appearance.
