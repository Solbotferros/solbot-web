In this project, we fabricated a custom metal coffee table for a home in El Masnou.

The aim was to create a functional piece that would fit the style of the space, combining materials and proportions to achieve a balanced and visually light result. A metal structure with straight lines and a minimalist design was chosen, providing solidity without making the overall piece feel heavy.

The table was made by adapting its dimensions and finishes to the existing environment, ensuring natural integration with the furniture and materials in the living room. This type of piece requires precision in execution and special attention to detail, as any deviation directly affects the final aesthetic.

The result is a simple and functional custom coffee table that integrates perfectly into the space and reinforces the character of the room while still standing out as a feature piece.
