In this project, we carried out the installation of a metal structure for the extension of a parking ramp in Badalona.

The intervention required adapting new structural elements to an existing concrete base, ensuring proper load transfer and overall stability. To achieve this, metal profiles and supports were incorporated, designed to integrate with the geometry of the ramp and the built environment.

Special attention was paid to the anchoring points and the alignment of the elements, ensuring reliable structural performance in a high-use environment. This type of work requires precise execution and correct adaptation to the real conditions on site.

The result is a solid and functional structural extension that optimises the parking area while maintaining the safety and stability of the system.
