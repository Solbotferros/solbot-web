In this project, we carried out the fabrication and installation of a custom metal pergola for the outdoor patio of a home in the Maresme.

The aim was to create a wide, resistant and functional shaded area, making good use of the available patio space without visually overloading the surroundings. For this reason, a metal structure supported by vertical posts was designed, with a layout intended to provide stability and properly cover the outdoor living area.

The structure was fabricated by adapting its dimensions, supports and finishes to the real characteristics of the space. Special attention was paid to levelling, profile alignment and the correct fixing of the posts, ensuring solid and durable performance in an outdoor environment.

In addition to its structural function, the pergola integrates with the style of the home and allows natural shading elements, such as reed screening or lightweight coverings, to be incorporated while maintaining a warm Mediterranean atmosphere.

The result is a practical, resistant and well-integrated metal pergola that improves the comfort of the patio and makes the outdoor space more enjoyable with greater protection from the sun.
