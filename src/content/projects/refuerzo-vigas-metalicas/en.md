In this project, we carried out the installation of metal beams for the structural reinforcement of a home in Cabrils.

The intervention was carried out on an existing wooden structure, incorporating metal beams to improve the strength and stability of the whole assembly. This type of work requires proper adaptation to the original structure, respecting its characteristics and ensuring suitable integration of the new elements.

Special attention was paid to the on-site fit and alignment of the beams, ensuring an even distribution of loads and a structurally solid result. In addition, the aim was to maintain the aesthetic coherence of the space, integrating the new metal elements with the original character of the home.

The result is a safe and durable structural solution that reinforces the stability of the home without altering its essence or interior aesthetics.
