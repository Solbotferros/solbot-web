In this project, we carried out the fabrication and installation of custom metal railings and security grilles for the façade of a building in Badalona.

The intervention required a uniform solution for different window openings and balconies, maintaining a consistent aesthetic across the entire façade. For this reason, a sober and functional design with straight lines and vertical bars was used, providing security without disrupting the appearance of the building.

Each piece was custom-made according to the dimensions of each opening, adjusting proportions, fixings and finishes to achieve clean integration with the existing joinery and construction frames. This type of work requires precision in measurement and installation, especially when repeated elements are installed across different floors and heights.

The result is a discreet, resistant and well-integrated metal solution that improves the building’s security while maintaining an orderly and uniform appearance across the entire façade.
