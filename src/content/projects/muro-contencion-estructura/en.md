In this project, we carried out the installation of a metal structure to reinforce a retaining wall at a home in Cabrils.

The intervention aimed to improve ground stability and reinforce an existing wall subjected to lateral earth pressure. To achieve this, metal posts and beams were incorporated to stiffen the assembly and distribute the loads more effectively across the structure.

This type of work requires proper adaptation to the existing construction, as well as precision in the placement and anchoring of each element, since the structural performance of the system depends on it.

The result is a solid and durable solution that improves the safety of the surroundings and ensures the long-term stability of the wall.
